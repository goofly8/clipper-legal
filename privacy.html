Clipper 2.0

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026

Clipper 2.0 is a desktop application. It has no backend server of its own and does not create an account for you. This page explains what data the App handles and where it goes.

Short version. Your video stays on your computer. Audio or text leaves it only if you turn on a cloud recognition or AI analysis provider. Publishing sends the finished clip to the platform you chose — only after you press the publish button.

1. Data processed on your computer

  • Video and audio files you select, plus the clips, captions and project files created from them.
  • App settings and history of actions.
  • Platform access tokens, encrypted with Windows DPAPI and stored in the App’s data folder.

2. Cloud processing (only if you enable it)

  • Speech recognition: if you choose a cloud provider instead of the built-in local model, the audio track of the video is sent to that provider (for example DeepInfra or OpenAI) to obtain a transcript.
  • Moment selection: if you use an AI model, the transcript and video metadata (title, duration, scene marks) are sent to the provider you configured (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI or DeepSeek) to choose clip moments.

These services receive only what is needed for the task. Their own privacy policies apply.

3. Publishing

When you confirm publishing, the App uploads the clip and the text you approved to the selected platform through its official API: YouTube (Google), TikTok, or Instagram (Meta). No other party receives the file. The platform’s privacy policy governs the published content.

4. What the App does not do

  • Does not collect analytics or telemetry.
  • Does not upload your video to the developer’s servers — there are none.
  • Does not read your accounts beyond the permissions you grant during sign-in.
  • Does not share data with advertisers.

5. Retention and deletion

All project data stays in the App’s data folder until you delete it. Deleting a project removes its files. Access tokens can be revoked at any time from your platform account settings (Google, TikTok, Meta); you may also delete the App’s data folder to remove them locally.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.
