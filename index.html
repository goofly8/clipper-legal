Clipper 2.0

Long videos become vertical clips.

A Windows desktop app that finds the strongest moments in long videos and streams, cuts them into 9:16 clips with captions, and publishes them to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels through official APIs — after you approve each clip.

Finds the momentsTranscript, hooks and scene analysis pick the parts worth posting.
Keeps faces in frameFace-aware cropping keeps the speaker centred in vertical video.
Captions that fitBurned-in subtitles with fonts, colours and live preview of the final frame.
Publishes with consentPrivate by default; nothing leaves the computer until you press publish.
Runs locally on Windows. Video is processed on your computer; cloud speech recognition and AI moment selection are optional and can be turned off.
